How hard should it feel?

  • Effort 5 to 6 out of 10. You can talk in short sentences, but you could not sing.
  • Stop a set 2 reps before your form breaks. Ugly reps are where knees and backs get hurt.
  • Move up a phase only when the top of every rep range feels easy for a full week.
  • Muscle soreness for a day or two is normal. Sharp joint pain is not: switch to the easier version.
  • Stop and get checked for chest pain or pressure, unusual breathlessness, dizziness or palpitations. If you have heart disease, uncontrolled BP, diabetes on insulin or major joint problems, clear the plan with your doctor first.

Why these moves: low-impact work (no jumping, no burpees) keeps load off knees and hips while strength improves. Strength sessions three days a week, on non-consecutive days, with walking on the others, is the pattern most beginner programs use.

How the maths works

BMR uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Maintenance (TDEE) is BMR times your activity factor. One kilogram of body fat is treated as about 7,700 kcal, so 0.5 kg a week needs a deficit of about 550 kcal a day. Intake never goes below 1,500 kcal (men) or 1,200 kcal (women). Exercise is not added back to your food budget; it makes the deficit deeper instead. Food values are typical home portions and will be off by 10 to 20 percent, so the weekly weigh-in is the real judge.
